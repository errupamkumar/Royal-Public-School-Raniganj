About the school

A Tradition of Excellence in Raniganj

Since 2005, Royal Public School has been a familiar name for families in Raniganj and the villages around it.

We are a co-educational school following the CBSE curriculum. Children attend as day scholars or stay on campus as residential students, which helps families who live farther away.

Our campus on Road No. 6 in Kalawati Nagar, Hasanpur brings classrooms, a big assembly ground, hostel and activities together, so learning continues beyond the textbook.

RPS presents

Royal Coaching Centre

Run by Royal Public School in Kalawati Nagar, near Satsang Mandir, for students who want extra guidance.

  • A team of experienced teachers for Classes 6 to 10
  • Separate subject teachers for each subject
  • Preparation for Navodaya, Netarhat and Simultala entrance exams
  • 24-hour study environment, with teachers staying in the hostel
  • Separate hostel arrangements for boys and girls, clean drinking water and 24-hour electricity
